Alp capabilities

The right tools for meaningful interaction.

Designed around the capabilities researchers use to study human behavior, AI, and social robotics.

Our first robot brings conversation, visual expression, and custom applications into one developing platform. Each capability has a clear purpose and a clear development status.

Meet Alp ↗

Communicate in more than one way

Build the interaction around the question.

These are the intended capabilities of Alp. Prototype work establishes parts of the foundation; the complete robot and integrated experience are under development.

Voice and conversation

In development · Audio capture prototyped

Develop spoken prompts and dialogue-driven activities for studying how people converse with a physical robot. Audio capture has been demonstrated in development hardware; the integrated listening, response, and speech experience is still being built.

Expressive visual interface

In development

Use facial display content and visual cues to communicate attention, feedback, and interaction state. Our expressive interface work aims to give researchers another way to shape how an activity feels and is understood.

Interactive touchscreen

In development

Pair conversation with study instructions, photo prompts, choices, and custom visual content. The planned front touchscreen provides a complementary interaction surface for experiences that need more than speech.

Perception-ready design

In development · Image capture prototyped

Bring camera inputs into researcher-defined applications. Image capture has been demonstrated on development hardware; integrated perception capabilities and their application interfaces continue to evolve.

A shared application foundation

Keep the experiment. Refine the experience.

Software is being developed alongside the robot so a new research idea can become a reusable application.

Custom applications

Prototype foundation · Expanding interfaces

Define study-specific prompts, interaction sequences, and application behavior. Early runtime and developer-tool prototypes support application execution; broader standardized robot interfaces are under development.

Application management

Prototype workflows · Integration in progress

Package, validate, install, and refine compatible applications through a more consistent workflow. Packaging and deployment prototypes exist; connecting the full web platform with the developing robot remains active engineering work.

Planned capability

New settings for interaction.

A discreet mobile base is part of Alp's intended first-generation design. Indoor movement could support future studies about a robot's positioning and physical presence.

Mobility has not been demonstrated. The base, movement control, and integrated behavior require development and validation.
