Meet Alp.

HumanFrame's humanoid robotic platform, designed around the spaces, interactions and proportions people already understand.

Interaction

Designed
to connect.

Attention can begin with something simple: a glance, a cue, a sense of presence. Alp explores expression through a minimal eye system and physical orientation.

Humanoid mobility

Built for a
human world.

Human environments are shaped around the way people move. Alp's intended bipedal form follows that same frame, from articulated hips to grounded feet.

Physical presence

Motion with purpose.

Arms, posture and orientation are part of the interaction. HumanFrame is developing an articulated platform whose movement can communicate as well as move.
