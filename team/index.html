Engineering leadership

Connecting the experience to the engineering.

Adrian Wadhwa · CEO

Adrian's background spans social robotics, conversational AI, embedded application systems, and enterprise technology infrastructure. In earlier RoboCare Lab work, he built a Pepper-based conversational reminiscence prototype connecting speech input, AI-generated responses, synthesized voice, a tablet interface, and robot behaviors.

At HumanFrame, he leads development of the runtime, SDK, and application-deployment foundation while coordinating software and robotics integration. That combination of research experience and practical systems development shapes the team's research-first direction.

Software leadership

Jacob Camoin

CTO

Jacob Camoin builds the software that brings HumanFrame Robotics' robot to life. He works across the systems that connect conversation, applications, and robot behavior, turning complex technology into interactions that feel natural and useful.

At HumanFrame Robotics, Jacob helps shape the robot's software from the ground up. His work combines technical range with a clear focus on the people who will use it, helping create a platform that is capable, approachable, and ready to grow.

Robotics leadership

Rudy Arsenault

Head of Electronics

Rudy Arsenault leads the electronics behind HumanFrame Robotics’ robot. He designs the hardware that lets it see, hear, speak, and move. A senior Robotics Engineering major at Worcester Polytechnic Institute, Rudy owns the robot’s electrical architecture from start to finish: power distribution, embedded computing, and the cameras, microphones, speakers, display, and motors that let it interact with people face to face.

His work turns ambitious ideas into hardware that is reliable, safe, and well integrated, and he never loses sight of who it’s for: people living with dementia and the caregivers who support them. Rudy pairs an engineer’s rigor with a builder’s hands-on instincts, and he believes the best robots come from teams of people with different backgrounds working toward one goal. He’s helping build that kind of team at HumanFrame Robotics.

Hardware and mechatronics leadership

Theron Boozer

Head of Hardware and Mechatronics

Theron's background spans control theory, multi-robot systems, mechatronics design, and human-robot interaction theory. With research contributions in artificial intelligence safety expected to be published at HRI 2027, he brings experience designing across mechanical systems, computer architectures, and human-computer interfaces.

At HumanFrame, he works to ensure every encounter feels natural and every robot is built to a high standard. His experience in prototype design and manufacturing makes him a strong lead for HumanFrame's mechanical design.
