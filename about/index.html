About HumanFrame

Technology should
adapt to people.

HumanFrame Robotics is building a personal humanoid platform around human spaces, human interaction and a software system that can evolve.

Our frame of reference

The world people already inhabit.

Homes, workplaces and shared spaces were shaped around human proportions and ways of moving. HumanFrame starts there.

Alp is the physical expression of that idea. HumanFrame Software and Platform extend it into a system developers can build on.
