About HumanFrame

Built across disciplines. Focused on people.

HumanFrame Robotics is developing accessible social robots and the software ecosystem that makes interactive research applications possible.

An early-stage robotics company.

Our first focus is Alp: an approachable research robot for universities, educators and human-robot interaction researchers. We’re developing its hardware, runtime, SDK and application tools together.

Affordability and developer accessibility guide that work. They are design objectives, with pricing and product availability still to be determined.

Why we’re building HumanFrame ↗

Experience informs the direction.

Earlier Pepper-based reminiscence research showed our team what it takes to connect a physical robot with a conversational application. That work informs HumanFrame’s focus on a reusable foundation for research.

HumanFrame is an independent venture. Prior lab research does not imply an institutional partnership or endorsement.
