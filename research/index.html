For researchers and educators

Make more room for the research.

Explore new human-robot interaction questions without rebuilding the same foundational tools each time.

HumanFrame is developing an accessible social robot and application framework for HRI labs, robotics courses, and human-centered computing research.

Start with your question

An adaptable platform for a specific experiment.

A social robotics study brings together interaction design, software behavior, and physical hardware. HumanFrame's aim is to make that foundation more reusable, so teams can spend more of their effort on the experience they want to investigate.

A consistent robot, configurable applications, and shared software interfaces are intended to reduce repeated integration work. Research teams would define their own study logic and adapt supported interaction capabilities as the platform develops.

We are looking for early conversations about practical lab and classroom needs. There are no announced institutional deployments or validated study outcomes.

The intended research workflow

From an interaction idea to an experiment.

A shared software foundation is being developed to connect the work between each stage.

Design an interaction

Define the question, prompts, participant actions, and feedback your activity needs.

Build an application

Express the interaction through an application using supported HumanFrame capabilities.

Run it on a robot

Package and deploy compatible software to bring the experience into a physical setting.

Iterate and learn

Refine the application and study design as you evaluate the interaction.

Application execution and deployment foundations exist in prototypes. The complete workflow on an integrated Alp research robot is a development goal.

An application grounded in earlier research

A conversation can begin with a memory.

Our photo-guided reminiscence application draws on earlier Pepper-based work in a RoboCare Lab setting. It offers a concrete example of combining a visual prompt with spoken interaction for dementia-care research.

The earlier Pepper prototype and the HumanFrame adaptation are distinct: the adaptation to our developing platform is in progress.

A platform for your methodology.

HumanFrame is developing interaction tools, rather than a complete study-management system. Study design, ethics review, participant consent, and data governance remain responsibilities for each research project.

Research-data collection interfaces, session analytics, and export tools are not being presented as existing platform capabilities.
