Alp · In development

A robot for studying interaction.

Alp is HumanFrame’s first social research robot, bringing an approachable physical design together with an evolving application framework.

The first generation

Designed around the interaction.

The intended platform combines speech, a digital face, camera-based inputs and a touchscreen for custom research experiences. Integrated hardware and interaction capabilities remain in development.

Indoor mobility is planned for the first generation and has not been demonstrated. Alp is not currently available to purchase.

Meet Alp in detail ↗

Hardware and software, developed together.

HumanFrame is building the runtime, SDK and application environment alongside the robot. Our aim is to provide a reusable foundation for experiments, coursework and new interactive applications.
